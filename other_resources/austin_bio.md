Austin Omogiate is an MBA candidate at Baylor University with close to five years of experience leading strategic projects and operations across the retail distribution and consumer packaged goods (CPG) industry. His work focuses on operations strategy, digital transformation, and market growth, partnering with cross-functional teams to improve commercial performance and operational efficiency.

Prior to Baylor, Austin led strategic projects at Multipro Consumer Products Limited, one of Africa's largest FMCG distribution companies, where he worked across supply chain, logistics, sales, and technology teams to strengthen distribution networks and support market expansion.

His experience includes:
- Designing go-to-market strategies that increase product availability and market share
- Optimizing distribution and logistics to reduce operational costs
- Resolving operational bottlenecks in product rollouts
- Leveraging digital platforms and data analytics to improve customer acquisition and retention
- Strengthening collaboration across commercial and operational teams
- Developing processes that reduce risk and improve efficiency

At Baylor, Austin is expanding his expertise in global supply chain operations, AI-driven digital transformation, and business strategy while exploring opportunities to apply analytics and operational excellence to complex business challenges.

He enjoys connecting with professionals working in supply chain, operations, strategy, and technology-driven business transformation.
